Crafting stylized worlds with narrative depth.

I'm Yilin An, a student in the Visual Game and Media Design Master of Arts program at Royal Danish Academy in Copenhagen.

I'm a 3D Visual and full-process game designer who crafts immersive worlds grounded in psychology, user experience, and environmental storytelling. My work often starts in my sketchbook, where I seek to understand the beauty of subjects and objects through form and structure, then proceeds to digital 3D and concept environmental design that translates that beauty into the vivid and stylized worlds I create.
